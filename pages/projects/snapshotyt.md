A lightweight extension that lets you drop your own titled timestamps on the YouTube progress bar while watching lectures, talks, and conferences. A flag button and a keyboard shortcut mark the moment, ticks show on the bar, and a list panel jumps back to any of them. Everything is saved per video and stays in the browser, with no account and no tracking.

Currently building toward a YouTube indexing and bookmarking tool that writes smart notes. The timestamps you mark will carry weight, so summaries emphasize the parts you flagged as important, and are sent straight to your note-taking app.
